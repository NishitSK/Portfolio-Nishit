Scheduler · Forecasting · Cloud

Carbon-Aware AI Workload Scheduler

A two-phase, SLA-first scheduler that decides where and when cloud jobs run. Phase 1 hard-filters regions on latency and capacity; Phase 2 minimises emissions across the survivors using a full energy model (CPU × TDP × exec time × PUE × carbon intensity).

Delay-tolerant jobs get joint spatial + temporal shifting with a 4-hour look-ahead; latency-sensitive jobs shift across space only. Live carbon intensity comes from the Electricity Maps API with MongoDB caching; forecasting benchmarked with LSTM vs ARIMA vs persistence on 4 years of per-zone grid data, tested fully out-of-sample on every season of 2025. An RL scheduler is benchmarked against the rule-based one, with a FastAPI analytics layer and React dashboard on top.
